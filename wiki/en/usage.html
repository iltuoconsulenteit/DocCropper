Using DocCropper

Home Usage
  1. Select Upload File to choose images or drag them on the page. On mobile you may switch to Camera and capture several photos.
  2. (Pro) Import PDF files to add each page to the gallery.
  3. Adjust the four corner handles or double click to detect edges automatically.
  4. Use the brightness and contrast sliders. (Pro) Convert images to grayscale or black & white and skip blank pages. You can restore color later with the Color button.
  5. Press Process Image to crop each photo.
  6. (Pro) Drag thumbnails to reorder them.
  7. Choose Sign under a page to upload or draw your signature and press Save. The Free edition keeps a watermark.
  8. Click Create PDF. Use Preview layout to visualize the arrangement. (Pro) Use Remote Sign to apply a digital signature, then share via WhatsApp Web or Email.
