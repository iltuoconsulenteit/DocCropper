Using DocCropper

The interface now sports a Material design look with cleaner buttons.

Home Usage
  1. Select Upload File to choose images or drag them on the page. Up to ten files are accepted at once (limit configurable in settings). On mobile you may switch to Camera and capture several photos.
  2. (Pro) Import PDF files to add each page to the gallery.
  3. Adjust the four corner handles or double click to detect edges automatically.
  4. Use the brightness and contrast sliders. (Pro) Convert images to grayscale or black & white, skip blank pages and remove backgrounds. You can restore color later with the Color button and fine-tune background removal with the threshold picker.
  5. Flip or invert images if they were scanned mirrored or upside-down.
  6. Press Process Image to crop each photo.
  7. (Pro) Drag thumbnails to reorder them.
  8. Use the pen button below a page to upload or draw your signature and press Save. The Free edition keeps a watermark.
  9. Click Create PDF. Choose a compression level (Low, Medium or Extreme with custom JPEG quality). The layout preview updates as you adjust options. (Pro) Use Remote Sign to apply a digital signature, then share via WhatsApp Web or Email.
