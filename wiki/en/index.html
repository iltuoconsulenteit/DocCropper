DocCropper Guide

Home Usage

DocCropper lets you capture or upload document images, correct their perspective and export them as a single PDF file.

Key Features

  • Multi-image upload with drag and drop
  • Camera capture on mobile devices
  • Interactive cropping with perspective correction
  • Brightness and contrast adjustment
  • PDF export with layout options
  • Optional sharing of processed images

See the Usage section for a step by step guide.
