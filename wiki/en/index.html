DocCropper Guide

Home Usage

DocCropper lets you capture or upload document images, correct their perspective and export them as a single PDF file.

Key Features

  • Multi-image upload with drag & drop (configurable limit)
  • Camera capture on mobile devices or choose files
  • Import PDF pages directly into the gallery (Pro)
  • Auto-detect edges with a double click
  • Interactive cropping with perspective correction
  • Brightness and contrast adjustment
  • Remove backgrounds with an adjustable threshold and restore originals (Pro)
  • Flip pages horizontally or invert upside-down scans
  • Convert images to grayscale or black & white (Pro)
  • Skip blank pages during PDF import (Pro)
  • Reorder pages by dragging thumbnails (Pro)
  • Add a signature image or drawing before export (Free, watermark applied)
  • Sign from your phone via QR code (Pro)
  • Compress and export PDFs, then share via WhatsApp Web or Email (Pro)

See the Usage section for a step by step guide.

Features marked "(Pro)" require a Pro or Full license. The Free edition allows basic signatures with a watermark and is limited to five images.
