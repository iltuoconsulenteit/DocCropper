Using DocCropper

Home Usage

DocCropper lets you upload up to ten images at once (limit configurable), fix their perspective and export them as a PDF.

The header can display a rotating banner of images listed in banner_images within settings.json. Place the files in static/slide/ and optionally include a {{lang}} placeholder in each filename to load localized versions.

  1. Drag images onto the page or use Upload File. Mobile devices can switch to the camera and, after cropping, tap Apply & Add to capture another photo.
  2. Adjust corner handles or double click to auto-detect edges.
  3. Fine-tune brightness/contrast, convert to grayscale or black & white, remove backgrounds and flip pages if needed.
  4. Press Process Image to crop, then add signatures or drawings.
  5. Choose page layout, optionally select a PDF/A version, and pick a compression level (Low, Medium, Extreme with custom JPEG quality) before creating the final PDF for download or sharing.
